## Category: Getting Started

### 1. What is Farmeco?

Farmeco is the modern marketplace for buying and selling livestock and poultry. We connect buyers with verified local farms for cattle, goats and sheep, pigs, poultry, and feed & supplies.

How it works in four steps:

1. Browse verified listings — filter by species, breed, health records and location. Every farm is location-verified before they can sell.
2. Review health paperwork — vaccination logs, vet reports and DNA results travel with every listing.
3. Delivery to your gate — licensed hauliers with biosecure crates bring your animals within the delivery window you pick.
4. Settle in with support — every order includes a 7-day health guarantee and direct access to the seller.

### 2. Do I need an account?

Yes, a free account is required to buy or sell. With an account you can message sellers, save items to your wishlist, place orders and track delivery.

## Category: Buying

### 3. How do I buy an animal?

1. Browse the marketplace and filter by species, breed, health records or location.
2. Open a listing to review the animal's health paperwork and message the seller.
3. Add to cart and go to checkout.
4. Choose a delivery window and pay by card or cash on delivery.
5. The seller will reach out within 24 hours to schedule delivery.

### 4. Can I see the animal before I commit?

Yes. Many sellers offer video calls or on-farm visits. You can message any seller directly from a listing. We recommend a pre-purchase vet exam for breeding animals.

### 5. What if the animal arrives unhealthy?

Contact us within 48 hours with photos and any vet notes. Our support team will work with you and the seller to arrange treatment, a replacement or a refund under the health guarantee.

## Category: Health & Guarantees

### 6. What does the 7-day health guarantee cover?

If an animal shows signs of a serious illness or a pre-existing condition within 7 days of delivery, we will help you arrange a vet assessment and a replacement or refund. The full terms are shown at checkout.

### 7. Do animals come with health records?

Yes. Every listing carries its health paperwork — vaccination logs, vet reports and, where relevant, DNA or registration certificates — which you can review before you buy.

---

## Category: Delivery

### 8. How does live-animal delivery work?

We partner with licensed hauliers using biosecure crates. When you order, you pick a delivery window and the seller and haulier coordinate. Live animals are typically delivered within 3–5 days, depending on distance.

### 9. Do you deliver to my area?

We currently serve 40 states. During checkout you will see live delivery options for your postal code. Rural delivery is available through our network of regional hauliers.

## Category: Payments

### 10. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:

- Debit and credit cards (secure checkout via Paystack)
- Cash on delivery
- Bank transfer

Payments are encrypted and only released to the seller once the delivery is confirmed.

### 11. When is the seller paid?

Sellers are paid within 2 business days of confirmed delivery.

## Category: Selling

### 12. How much does it cost to sell?

Sellers keep 96% of every sale — we charge a flat 4% marketplace fee with no listing fees. There are no hidden charges, and sellers are paid within 2 business days of confirmed delivery.

### 13. How do I become a seller?

We onboard new farms every month. Get in touch through the contact page or our support channels and our seller team will walk you through listing, health records and delivery. Every farm location is verified before they can list.

### 14. How does Farmeco verify farms and animals?

Every seller's farm location is verified before they can list. Each animal carries its health paperwork — vaccination logs, vet reports and, where relevant, DNA or registration certificates — which buyers can review before purchase.
